What counts as a durable workflow

The metric is deliberately strict so a spike or raw activity cannot qualify. A workflow counts only if all three hold:

  • 1 · recursAt least 3 runs in 14 days. A one-off or a demo does not register.
  • 2 · persistsPresent across two consecutive windows. This kills novelty pops: the spike that fades in window 2 fails here.
  • 3 · ties to outcomeLinked to a user-side outcome, not raw agent activity. Activity often rises right before a budget cut, so counting activity reads a decline as health.
