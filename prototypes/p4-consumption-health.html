What counts as a durable workflow

The metric is deliberately strict so a spike or raw activity can't qualify. A workflow counts only if all three hold:

  • 1 · recurs At least 3 runs in 14 days. A one-off or a demo doesn't register.
  • 2 · persists Present across two consecutive windows. This kills novelty pops — the spike that fades in window 2 fails here.
  • 3 · ties to outcome Linked to a user-side outcome (a finished artifact, a shipped decision), not raw agent activity — activity often rises right before a budget cut as a team scrambles, so counting activity reads a decline as health.
Two durable modes
Finished work-product
Account uses it to produce a deliverable that leaves the product — a contract draft, a report, a customer reply. Outcome = the artifact shipped.
Recurring thinking-partner
Account returns to reason through decisions on a cadence. Outcome = the decision made, measured by repeat sessions on the same job-to-be-done.
