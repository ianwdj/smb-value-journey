Value-realization engine · architecture

Six primitives. Three scale stages. One pipeline.

The same core chain — telemetry → stall detector → next-workflow ranker → teach generator → eval gate → delivery surface — is reused at every stage. What changes between stages is who approves delivery and, at enterprise only, an added connectors layer that maps the org and ties usage to the work it replaced. Detection, ranking, drafting, and measurement stay autonomous throughout; pricing and contract actions never are.

core primitive (reused, unchanged) eval gate delivery surface human approval (V1 only) connectors (enterprise only)
STAGE 1

V1 — Internal (CS pod supervises)

What stayed the same: all six primitives, in order. What changed vs. nothing — this is the baseline. Delivery is human-approved: a small CS pod reviews each drafted teach before it reaches an account, the way the inbound agent earned autonomy from 60% to 98% before acting on its own. Access is in-product behavioral signal only — no connectors.

STAGE 2

SMB — Direct (user consent, no human)

What stayed the same: the identical six primitives, same order, same in-product-only data access. What changed: the human approval step is gone — once the eval pass-rate clears its bar, the eval gate opens the delivery surface directly, and the SMB user accepts or dismisses each action with one-click consent. No new components; the engine simply earned the right to act without the pod.

STAGE 3

Enterprise — same primitives + connectors

What stayed the same: the six primitives, unchanged in shape and order — the same engine that ran internally and direct-to-SMB. What changed: a connectors layer (granted by IT and legal) feeds telemetry with an org map, and the delivery surface now emits work-replaced ROI — tying usage to the function it replaced, the answer consultants are paid for today. Delivery stays consent-based; no human approval returns.

Design exploration — illustrates how one primitive set scales across stages. Not a shipped architecture. Autonomy boundaries (pricing, billing, contract actions never autonomous) hold at every stage.
